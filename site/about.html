About RoachFinder

Mission

Provide a selection of NFL spread wagers for weeks 2–16 of the NFL season. Ideally, the algorithm will decide winning wagers ≥ 52.38% of the time. This will, in the long term, result in being +$ on the season.

How to Use It

It is very important that this algorithm is followed in the manner in which it was designed to be utilized. Essentially, there are 2 key factors that must be followed.

Key Factors

1. Wager the same unit/monetary amount for each matchup within that current week. This can be by your discretion or use the Kelly Calculator.

2. Gamble on the same number of games week over week.

If you do this AND the algorithm performs with an accuracy greater than ~53%, you will have made a profit at the season end.

Kelly Criterion

A Kelly Criterion Calculator has been implemented to optimize your betting strategy based on actual performance data. This mathematical approach replaces the fixed-unit betting system with dynamic position sizing that adapts to your bankroll and our proven win rate.

Formula
f = (bp − q) / b

f = Fraction of bankroll to wager

b = Net odds received (profit per dollar wagered)

p = Probability of winning (current win rate)

q = Probability of losing (1 − p)

The Kelly Criterion maximizes the logarithm of wealth over time, ensuring optimal capital growth while minimizing the risk of ruin. Unlike fixed betting, Kelly adjusts your wager size based on your edge and the odds offered.

Weekly Implementation

1. Input your current bankroll at the start of each week

2. The calculator uses cumulative win rate to determine optimal bet size

3. Bet the calculated amount on each recommended game

4. Adjust bankroll weekly based on wins/losses and recalculate

Important: It is recommended to use fractional Kelly (1/2 or 1/4) to reduce volatility. The calculator provides both full Kelly and conservative options to match your risk tolerance while maintaining mathematically optimal growth rates.

Roach's Disclaimer

The Kelly Criterion is simply a formula to find the optimal fraction of capital to bet given opportunities to maximize long-term growth by minimizing risk to the highest degree. I will be privately tracking the performance of the Kelly Criterion vs Fixed Unit betting over the course of the season; you guys will only see Fixed Unit as usual. In order for the Kelly Criterion to be effective, it requires a positive expectation — a definite advantage. Since the RoachFinder does not have a true edge WoW, we have to speculate it provides a positive EV, so the mathematical reality of its advantage is really more of an assumption. This is just a tool for you all to give an idea of bankroll management week over week. For that matter, the assumption of 'p' (our probability of winning) is based on last year's win rate. In that same chord, q is 1−p.
